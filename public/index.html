How to race

Everyone plays at once. Clear all 40 cards to win. Each deck has a 1 through 10 in yellow, blue, red, and green, with one tool printed on every back.

↑ Rise
Play one number higher. After 10 comes 1.
＝ Match
Play the same color. Any number works.
↓ Fall
Play one number lower. After 1 comes 10.

Play a 1 to open a new pile. New piles cycle through Rise, Match, and Fall. Swipe the row or use the arrows to see more piles.

Tap a highlighted card or drag it to a pile. If it fits in several places, choose a pile. Each play draws a replacement while cards remain in your deck.

Select a card and tap Swap card to trade it for your next card. If nobody can play, the tops of the piles reset.
