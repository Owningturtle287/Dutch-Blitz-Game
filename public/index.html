How to race

Each player starts with 27 cards in the Wood pile, three face-up cards, and 10 cards in the Blitz pile. After the 10-second countdown, everyone plays at once.

  • Play a 1 to open a shared pile. By default, each next card must be one number higher and the same color. A 10 played on a 9 goes face down and closes the pile.
  • Drag a face-up card onto the table to play it on the nearest eligible pile. You can also tap a 1 to open a pile. When you play one of your three face-up cards, the top Blitz card fills its spot. Emptying Blitz ends the round.
  • Tap your Wood pile to flip three cards when enough remain. Play the exposed card to uncover the next one. You can wait for a match or flip again. The unplayed Wood cards are shuffled as soon as the stock runs out.
  • Score one point per card played to the table and lose two points per card left in Blitz. A game adds up scores over its chosen rounds. Free Play scores each round on its own.
